# Known Failure Modes

- Dense slide pages get compressed into one takeaway paragraph instead of being unpacked step by step.
- Formulas are copied into LaTeX without explaining what each symbol means.
- Code appears in the note, but the surrounding explanation does not describe the update rule or branch logic.
- Figures are inserted because they look important, but the caption does not explain what the reader should learn from them.
- A figure derived from the video lacks time provenance.
- Coverage ledgers classify units as required, but the note leaves them unclassified or silently omits them.
- Repeated agenda or logistics pages are dropped without recording an omission.
- Writer prose drifts away from canonical evidence when `transcript.txt` or `official.txt` is used instead of structured evidence.
