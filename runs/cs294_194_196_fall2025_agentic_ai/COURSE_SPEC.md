## Coverage goals

- Primary textbook spine must follow the Fall 2025 Berkeley Agentic AI lecture order.
- Missing recordings must not block completion; slides and official readings must be used directly when recordings are absent.
- Supplemental courses may strengthen explanations on:
  - LLM foundations and reasoning
  - planning and search
  - memory and long-horizon agents
  - coding agents
  - robotics and embodied agents
  - web automation
  - scientific discovery

## Non-negotiable constraints

- Coverage-first over summary quality.
- Every lecture should exist as a standalone chapter before merge.
- All source gaps must be recorded in omission logs.
- Supplemental material must be explicitly manifested.
